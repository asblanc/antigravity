# Design System: IVOIRE BUSINESS CLUB (IBC)
**Project ID:** 12821775708247337648

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Premium, elegant, and luxurious aesthetic inspired by private clubs and high-end tourism. The vibe is "Prestige & Excellence" with a mix of deep héraldique greens and royal gold accents. It balances heritage (serif typography) with modern efficiency (clean Inter UI).

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
* **Vert IBC Foncé (#1B5E35):** Primary identity color, used for backgrounds, headers, and major structural elements.
* **Or IBC Principal (#C9A84C):** Primary accent color, used for CTA buttons, premium borders, and important UI highlights.
* **Or IBC Lumineux (#F0C040):** Highlight color for hover states, interactive feedback, and subtle gradients.
* **Crème/Ivoire (#FAF5E9):** Soft background for cards and secondary content areas to reduce contrast vs pure white.
* **Blanc Cassé (#FEFCF5):** Main page background to provide a warm, premium feel.
* **Vert Très Foncé (#0D3B22):** Deepest green used for footers and high-contrast typography on light backgrounds.

## 3. Typography Rules
* **Headings (H1, H2):** "Playfair Display" (Serif). Weights 700-900. Slogans use italics. Letter-spacing -0.02em for a dense, high-fashion look.
* **Sub-titles (H3, H4):** "Cormorant Garamond" (Serif). Weights 600-700.
* **Body Text:** "Inter" (Sans-serif). Weights 400-600. Line-height 1.6 for readability.
* **Interactive (Labels, Buttons, Tags):** "Inter" (Sans-serif). Weights 700-900. Uppercase with tracking 0.1em to 0.4em.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Rectangular or subtly rounded (`rounded-none` or `rounded-sm`). No heavy rounding to maintain a "Club" feel. Primary buttons use a Gold Gradient with Dark Green text.
* **Cards:** Cream background (#FAF5E9) with fine Gold borders (#C9A84C). Subtle diffuse shadows.
* **Navigation:** Transparent on Hero sections, transitioning to White/Green with Gold shadows on scroll. Links use a smooth Gold underline on hover.
* **Form Inputs:** Fine Gold borders, warm white backgrounds, and Inter typography.

## 5. Layout Principles
* **Whitespace:** Generous margins to emphasize "Luxury" and breathing room.
* **Grids:** Responsive columns (1 mobile → 3 desktop).
* **Animations:** Subtle fade-ins and slide-ups using Framer Motion and Intersection Observer.
* **Borders:** Extremely thin gold borders are the primary decorative element.
